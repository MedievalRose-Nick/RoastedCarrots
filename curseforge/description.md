# Roasted Carrots

Your carrot pile, finally worth eating. Cooks like a potato and fills you up more than one.

Cook a carrot in a **furnace, smoker, campfire or soul campfire** to get a **Roasted Carrot**.

| Food | Hunger | Saturation |
|---|---|---|
| Carrot | 3 | 3.6 |
| Baked Potato | 5 | 6 |
| **Roasted Carrot** | **6** | **12** |

High saturation means your health comes back faster after you eat.

## Works with Deepcore
Roasted Carrots in a Deep Storage Crate show their own picture. Deepcore is optional.

## Install
Open the `.mcaddon` file, then turn on both the behavior pack and the resource pack for your world.
