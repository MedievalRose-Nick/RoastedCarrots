Works in a furnace, smoker, campfire or soul campfire.

What one gives you

FoodHungerSaturation

Saturation is what makes your health come back quickly, and a Roasted Carrot gives twice as much as a baked potato.

Install

Download RoastedCarrots_v1.2.1.mcaddon and open it, or upload it to your server. Turn on both the behavior pack and the resource pack for your world.

Playing with Deepcore? Roasted Carrots in a Deep Storage Crate show their own picture. Deepcore is optional.
